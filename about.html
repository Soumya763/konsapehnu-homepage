ABOUT

Your wardrobe. Your personal stylist.

AI styling for the modern Indian man.

Most men own more clothes than they think they need, and still struggle to decide what to wear. Konsa Pehnu exists to solve that specific problem — not by selling you more clothes, but by helping you get more out of what you already own.

What we're building

Konsa Pehnu is an AI stylist for Indian men. You digitize your wardrobe by photographing the clothes you own, and the app helps you discover outfit combinations for real occasions — office, dates, weddings, festivals, casual days — using your own clothes instead of generic recommendations.

Why India-first

Most styling apps are built around Western wardrobes and Western occasions. Konsa Pehnu is built specifically around how Indian men actually dress — across kurtas, sherwanis and Western wear alike — and around Indian occasions like weddings, festivals and family functions, not just "smart casual" and "formal."

Where we are today

Konsa Pehnu is an early-stage product. The features described on this site — the digital wardrobe, AI outfit recommendations and the AI stylist chat — reflect what we're actively building toward. See the Features and Plans sections on the homepage for the most current picture of what's available.
